# Liturgical Form and Chiral Error Correction: An Integration-First Companion to Decoupled State Gating

## Abstract

Whereas falsification-driven epistemic architectures secure reliability by breaking candidate claims open to discard what fails, durable human communities face an orthogonal structural problem: transmitting shared meaning, ethical obligation, and collective coherence across generations without collapsing into dead literalism, brittle dogma, or performative status signaling. Written as the formal humanities and philosophical twin to *Decoupled Neuro-Symbolic State Gating*, this essay investigates the **integration-first chiral enantiomer** of the four-stage epistemic state machine: **Substrate (Offertory) → Binding Commit (Anaphora) → Opening/Fraction → Assimilation (Communion)**. Maintaining a strict boundary against vacuous universal-predicate collapse ($H(P) \approx 0$), we reject any naïve functorial identification between empirical science and religious liturgy. Instead, we show that Socratic *elenchus* / Popperian science and classical Eucharistic liturgy constitute **non-superimposable chiral enantiomers**—sharing an identical fourfold relational geometry while inverting parity at Stage 3 (truth-value *falsification* versus non-falsifiable sacramental *fraction*) and Stage 4 (cleared register `0` versus Augustinian communal assimilation). We trace this chiral architecture across four interlocking motifs: (1) the late-antique rivalry between the healing cult of Asclepius (including Peratic Ophiuchus star-lore) and early Christology; (2) the hermeneutic re-indexing of ordinary ritual maintenance into shared celebration at Cana and the reversal of biological digestion in the Eucharist; (3) **the Clasp**, in which opposite chiral orientations lock into a closed tensile ring uniting mundane maintenance with shared meaning; and (4) ethical chirality, where unobserved-cost ablations filter overt performative signaling (subject to internalized self-signaling limits) while closed-loop infallibility destroys both empirical inquiry and authentic moral agency.

## 1. Introduction: Two Hands of Cultural Transmission

Every civilization that endures across multiple generations confronts two simultaneous, structurally opposed failure modes. The first is **epistemic drift**: without a disciplined mechanism for testing assertions against external friction and pruning falsehoods, collective memory fills with flattering consensus, unchecked speculation, and recurring errors. The companion systems monograph (*Decoupled Neuro-Symbolic State Gating*) analyzes this first problem through a falsification-first state machine uniting Socratic *elenchus* (Vlastos, 1983), Popperian experimental testing (Popper, 1959), and defeasible relational state ledgers.

The second failure mode is **communal dissolution**: a society cannot live by falsification alone. Shared norms, mutual trust, bereavement, obligation to strangers, and intergenerational continuity are not predictive hypotheses awaiting experimental refutation; they are **integration structures** that bind autonomous individuals into a durable social body (Durkheim, 1912/1995; Rappaport, 1999). When a community loses its capacity to transmit shared meaning—or when its rituals ossify into mechanical compliance, superstitious literalism, or competitive moral display—social cohesion fractures just as surely as an ungated reasoning engine degrades under noise.

Already in the *Phaedrus* (274b–275e), Plato diagnosed the permanent hazard of externalized cultural memory: written inscription (*hypomnesis*) acts as an ambivalent *pharmakon* (at once remedy and poison) that preserves outer form while threatening to hollow out living recollection (*mneme*). External symbols cannot interpret themselves. If they are treated as self-executing magical formulas, liturgy degrades into dead literalism; if they are treated as competitive badges of virtue, ethics degrades into theater.

The central argument of this essay is that falsification-first inquiry and integration-first liturgy are neither identical nor mutually hostile. Rather, they stand to one another as **non-superimposable chiral enantiomers**. Borrowed strictly as a formal relational concept of orientation parity—two structures sharing the same connectivity and fourfold hand-geometry whose spatial handedness is inverted, and without claiming any literal geometric or biological tiling reduction—chirality explains why science and ritual look uncannily similar in outer sequence yet fail catastrophically whenever one is forced to perform the work of the other.

## 2. The Chiral Four-Stage Architecture: Sacramental Fraction vs. Empirical Falsification

### 2.1 Why Liturgy Cannot Be Collapsed into a Scientific Functor

In category theory, a genuine structural isomorphism requires a functor $F$ that preserves both identity morphisms and composition ($F(g \circ f) = F(g) \circ F(f)$; Mac Lane, 1998). In information theory, a discrete unweighted classification predicate $P: \mathcal{X} \to \{0, 1\}$ loses all discriminative power as its acceptance probability $p \to 1$, collapsing Shannon entropy and mutual information identically to zero ($H(P) = 0, I(P; X) = 0$; Shannon, 1948).

These two formal boundaries forbid a tempting but fatal simplification. At a surface level, classical Eucharistic liturgy—codified in its four-action shape by Justin Martyr (*First Apology*, chs. 65–67) and systematically analyzed by Dom Gregory Dix (1945) as **Taking (Offertory) → Blessing (Anaphora) → Breaking (Fraction) → Giving (Communion)**—appears to mirror the four-stage falsification protocol of Socratic dialectic and experimental science (**Substrate → Lock-In/Commit → Rupture/Falsification → State Integration**).

Yet attempting to fuse both under a single functor breaks decisively at **Stage 3**:

1. **In Socratic *elenchus* and empirical science**, Stage 3 breaks a *truth claim*. Deductive contradiction ($S \cup \{P\} \vdash \bot$) or experimental falsification ($\|\hat{y} - y_{\text{obs}}\| > \epsilon$) acts directly on the committed proposition so that false structure can be rejected, logged as a negative boundary, and replaced.
2. **In Eucharistic liturgy**, Stage 3 (*fraction*) breaks a *material vessel*—the consecrated loaf—precisely so that one common source can be spatially divided and distributed among many participants. The theological ontology of the rite is explicitly **immune to empirical falsification**; breaking the bread does not test or refute a hypothesis about wheat chemistry.

To equate physical bread-snapping with deductive refutation commits a verbal equivocation on the word *"breaking."* Worse, weakening Stage 3 from *truth-value breakability* down to a generic *"assemble → commit → open/divide → assign"* schema yields a vacuous $H(P) \approx 0$ template satisfied indiscriminately by commercial bakeries, TCP/IP packet demultiplexing, and corporate earnings reports. A rigorous humanities analysis must therefore **preserve the Stage 3 functorial break** rather than paper over it.

- **Importing Left-Handed immunity into Right-Handed inquiry** converts science or engineering into dogmatic orthodoxy: hypotheses become sacred totems immune to Stage 3 contradiction, generating closed-loop infallibility, verifier theater, and permanent institutional error.
- **Importing Right-Handed literalism into Left-Handed liturgy** converts communal sacrament into counterfeit physics: ritual symbols are misread either as brute magical mechanics (yielding fundamentalist literalism or ancient charges of ritual cannibalism) or as failed empirical propositions to be discarded once they fail a laboratory assay.

Each hand preserves its integrity only so long as the mirror boundary between them remains intact.

## 3. Structural Enantiomers in Late Antiquity: Asclepius, Ophiuchus, and Early Christology

Nowhere in cultural history is chiral parity—identical structural atoms configured with inverted ethical and cosmological handedness—more starkly documented than in the second- and third-century Mediterranean rivalry between the Hellenic healing cult of **Asclepius** and early **Christology**.

### 3.1 Identical Structural Atoms, Competing Configurations

As Emma and Ludwig Edelstein demonstrated in their monumental collection of ancient testimonies, Asclepius stood in late antiquity as *"the foremost antagonist of Christ"* (Edelstein & Edelstein, 1945, Vol. 2, p. 132). To an educated Greco-Roman observer, the structural overlap between the two figures was unmistakable rather than accidental:

1. **Lineage:** Both were venerated as born of a divine father (Apollo / God) and a mortal mother (Coronis / Mary).
2. **Domain and Titles:** Asclepius bore the official cult titles *Soter* (Savior) and *Philanthropos* (Lover of Humanity). Across great healing sanctuaries (*Asclepieia*) such as Epidaurus, Pergamon, and Aegae in Cilicia, supplicants underwent ritual purification and temple incubation (*koimesis*), sleeping in sacred dormitories to receive curative dreams or medical regimens documented on inscribed votive stelae (*iamata*; Edelstein & Edelstein, 1945).
3. **Boundary Crossing and Execution:** According to Greek myth, Asclepius transgressed the boundary between mortality and immortality by raising the dead, was struck down by Zeus's thunderbolt for violating cosmic order (*Moira*), and was subsequently deified and placed among the stars as **Ophiuchus**, the Serpent-Bearer.
4. **The Serpent and Medicine Vocabulary:** In the Gospel of John (3:14), Jesus explicitly correlates his own elevation with Moses lifting the bronze serpent (*Nehushtan*, Numbers 21:8–9) in the wilderness so that the bitten might look upon it and live—a visual motif immediately legible to Hellenistic audiences familiar with the serpent-entwined Rod of Asclepius.

Early Christian and pagan polemicists alike responded to this shared structural formula with acute **chiral discrimination**—treating the rival configuration not as a distant foreign deity, but as an inverted counterfeit binding the same cultural receptor site. Around 155 CE, **Justin Martyr** addressed Roman critics directly in his *First Apology* (chs. 21–22), noting that Christian claims of healing the lame, curing the blind, and raising the dead paralleled what Romans already believed of Asclepius, who *"though he was a great physician, was struck by a thunderbolt, and so ascended to heaven"* (Ante-Nicene Fathers / Dods trans.; cf. Barnard, 1997); yet Justin simultaneously dismissed the pagan parallel as preemptive demonic mimicry distorting prophetic scripture (*First Apology*, ch. 54).

A century later, in *Contra Celsum* (Book III, chs. 24–25; c. 248 CE), the Platonist critic **Celsus** inverted the charge: Asclepius, he argued, was the active, empirically accessible savior whose visible appearances and cures stood attested in Greek sanctuaries, whereas Jesus appeared an obscure provincial imitation. **Origen** countered in *Contra Celsum* III.24–25 not by denying Asclepian healing stories, but by stripping them of theological weight—arguing that bodily healing and foreknowledge are morally indifferent (*mesa*) feats performed by lower daemons for both righteous and wicked people alike, proving nothing of divinity or moral goodness (an anti-mercenary critique that complemented the classical mythic tradition—attested earlier in **Pindar**, *Pythian* 3.54–58; **Plato**, *Republic* III, 408b–c; and **Clement of Alexandria**, *Protrepticus* II.30—that Zeus struck Asclepius down precisely for accepting gold to raise a dead man). By the early second century, **Ignatius of Antioch** had already appropriated Asclepian medical vocabulary for the Eucharist itself, calling the one broken bread *"the medicine of immortality (*pharmakon athanasias*), the antidote that we should not die, but live forever in Jesus Christ"* (*Letter to the Ephesians*, 20.2; Ehrman, 2003). By the fourth century, imperial Christian historiography explicitly targeted Asclepius's sanctuaries as direct competitors: **Eusebius of Caesarea** records Emperor Constantine's dismantling of the celebrated Asclepieion at Aegae in Cilicia, condemning Asclepius as a **"destroyer of souls"** (*psycholethron*) drawing worshippers away from the true Savior (*Life of Constantine*, Book III, ch. 56; Cameron & Hall, 1999).

### 3.2 Second Temple Apocalypticism and Peratic Ophiuchus Synthesis

Why did two figures composed of such similar narrative atoms produce opposite social and theological vectors? The answer lies in their distinct ideological substrates. Asclepius operated within Hellenic hero-cult and civic medicine: restoration of biological equilibrium within an unchanged, hierarchically stratified cosmos governed by fate (*Moira*). Early Christology, by contrast, crystallized out of **Second Temple Jewish apocalypticism** (c. 200 BCE – 100 CE)—an ideological matrix born from the crisis of theodicy under Seleucid persecution and Roman occupation, partitioning history into *This Present Evil Age* (*Olam Ha-Zeh*) and *The Age to Come* (*Olam Ha-Ba*) and anticipating a transcendent "Son of Man" (Daniel 7, 12) who breaks the cosmic order rather than restoring equilibrium inside it.

The precise historical point where Second Temple apocalypticism, Greco-Roman star-lore, and early Christology fused into a single explicit diagram is preserved in the early third-century *Refutation of All Heresies* (*Elenchos*) attributed to **Hippolytus of Rome** (Books IV–V; Litwa, 2016). In Book IV, Hippolytus catalogs Greco-Roman celestial mythology (drawing heavily on Aratus's *Phaenomena*), detailing the northern constellations—Draco, Engonasin, and **Ophiuchus** (identified with Asclepius)—to expose astrology as human mythic projection. In Book V (chs. 12–18), he turns that catalog into an evidentiary indictment against the **Peratae**, a second-century Christian Gnostic sect that explicitly mapped the serpent held by Ophiuchus (*Serpens*) and the polar *Draco* onto the mediating cosmic *Logos* (Christ). Reading Genesis 3, Numbers 21, and John 3:14 through the northern night sky, the Peratae—alongside the closely related serpent-revering **Naassenes** (Book V, chs. 6.3–11.1), whose exposition (5.7) explicitly invokes **Hermes's golden wand / caduceus** (*Odyssey* 5.47–48) as the guide waking sleeping souls from material forgetfulness—re-indexed classical serpent and healing iconography from guardians of earthly cycles into apocalyptic liberators piercing astrological fate (*heimarmene*).

### 3.3 The Chiral Parity Inversion of Healing and Iconography

Mainstream proto-orthodox Christianity rejected Peratic astrological Gnosticism, yet preserved an even sharper **parity inversion** relative to the Asclepian sanctuary model:

*Table 2. Structural enantiomers in late antiquity: Hellenic Asclepian sanctuary healing vs. early Christian cost-bearing inversion.*

| Structural Axis | Left-Handed Configuration (Asclepius / Hellenic Sanctuary) | Right-Handed Configuration (Early Christology / Second Temple Apocalypticism) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Direction of Action** | **Immanent & Transactional:** The supplicant travels to a designated sacred precinct (*Asclepieion*), observes ritual purity, offers sacrifice or votive fee, and receives restored bodily equilibrium via incubation (*koimesis*). | **Incarnational & Cost-Bearing:** The healer enters the condition of the afflicted outside sacred purity boundaries, absorbing mortal suffering directly rather than dispensing cures from an insulated sanctuary. |
| **Status of the Cosmic Boundary** | **Preservation via Transgression:** Raising the dead violates cosmic hierarchy (*Moira*); Zeus's thunderbolt re-enforces the boundary between gods and mortals. | **Abolition via Self-Emptying:** Entering death voluntarily and rising from it overturns the boundary itself, inaugurating a new communal order rather than repairing the old hierarchy. |
| **Status of Public Record** | **Epigraphic Legibility (*Iamata*):** Stone cure testimonials inscribed publicly at Epidaurus advertise the sanctuary's efficacy and social prestige. | **Inverted Legibility (*Kenosis*):** Repeated injunctions to secrecy, concealed almsgiving, and inverted status (foot-washing, table fellowship with outcasts) resist transactional prestige. |
| **Iconographic Geometry** | **Single-Serpent Rough Staff:** An asymmetric, single-handed axis of clinical restoration (later visually conflated in popular culture with Hermes's bilateral two-serpent **Caduceus**, a balanced emblem of mediation and exchange). | **Elevated Antidote (*Nehushtan* / Cross):** Reconfiguring an emblem of venom or imperial deterrence into the vertical axis of communal healing and solidarity. |

Asclepius was therefore neither the literary prototype of Jesus nor an unrelated parallel. The two configurations operated as **cultural enantiomers** competing inside the same Mediterranean medium: identical narrative atoms, inverted parity regarding hierarchy, cost, and boundary preservation.

## 4. Re-Indexing Maintenance into Meaning: Cana, the Eucharist, and the Clasp

If late-antique Christology inverted the sanctuary transaction of Asclepius, its foundational domestic narratives—the **Wedding at Cana** (John 2:1–11) and the **Eucharist**—illustrate how integration-first liturgy transforms mundane, repetitive survival maintenance into high-bandwidth communal meaning without denying material reality.

### 4.1 Cana: Hermeneutic Re-Indexing of the Six Stone Purification Jars

Read through a brittle literalist lens—the very Right-Handed category error identified in Section 2.2—the Wedding at Cana appears as a brute-force suspension of physics, conjuring foreign carbon chains out of thin air to turn water into ethanol. Read through comparative structural hermeneutics, however, the episode performs an altogether different operation: **re-indexing the latent geometry of routine maintenance into shared celebration**.

The narrative's material anchors are explicit and structurally loaded:

- **The Six Stone Water Jars:** John specifies that the six stone jars stood ready *"for the Jewish rites of purification"* (John 2:6). Structurally, ritual washing water represents **static, repetitive external maintenance**—the unglamorous, cyclical work required to keep surfaces clean and boundaries intact. In Johannine numerology, six marks structural incompletion (one short of the sabbatical seven).
- **The Shared Structural Polarity (`H–O–H` vs. `R–O–H`):** Strictly as a conceptual and literary motif—never as an empirical chemical claim—ordinary water ($\text{H–O–H}$) and fermented vintage (ethanol, $\text{R–O–H}$, grown through sunlight, soil, crushing, and microbial time) share an underlying hydroxyl motif while inhabiting opposite functional registers: inert daily solvent versus festive, communal spirit.
- **Reversing Feast Entropy:** Crucially, the master of the banquet observes that hosts normally serve fine wine first and inferior wine once guests have grown dull, whereas here the finest vintage arrives last (John 2:10). Rather than discarding the stone jars of daily law and maintenance to import an alien vessel, the narrative draws directly *out of the purification jars themselves*—signifying that ordinary, disciplined maintenance is not the enemy of communal joy, but the exact vessel required to hold it.

### 4.2 The Eucharist: Cultured Nature and the Augustinian Reversal of Digestion

The Eucharist extends this re-indexing from wedding narrative into repeatable communal architecture. Crucially, classical liturgy does not consecrate raw, unworked elements (such as plain spring water or wild fruit); its substrate consists of **bread and wine**—cultured artifacts produced by collective agricultural labor, milling, crushing, yeast fermentation, and baking. Human toil and earthly matter are brought forward together at Stage 1 (*Offertory*), locked into canonical memory and public acceptance at Stage 2 (*Anaphora*; Rappaport, 1999), physically divided at Stage 3 (*Fraction*), and consumed at Stage 4 (*Communion*; Justin Martyr, *First Apology*, chs. 65–67; Dix, 1945).

Over two millennia, Christian theological traditions diverged sharply over the metaphysical ontology of consecration—ranging from Roman Catholic Aristotelian *transubstantiation* (accidents persisting while substance changes) and Eastern Orthodox apophatic *mysterion* (real change via *epiclesis* without scholastic mechanism), to Lutheran *sacramental union* ("in, with, and under"), Reformed *pneumatic presence*, and Zwinglian *symbolic memorial*. Historically, literalist misreadings of Eucharistic language even triggered second-century Roman accusations of *Thyestean banquets* (ritual cannibalism), compelling apologists such as Justin Martyr to publish plain descriptions of the bread-and-wine assembly to clear the community of criminal slander.

Across these metaphysical divergences, however, stands one unmistakable **structural inversion** articulated with surgical clarity by **Augustine of Hippo** in Book VII of the *Confessions* (397–400 CE / 1991, VII.10.16). In ordinary biological metabolism, digestion is an asymmetric act of ego-dominance: an organism breaks down external food and converts that foreign matter into its own tissue. Augustine describes the voice of the consecrated food reversing the arrow of assimilation:

> *"I am the food of the mature; grow then, and you will eat me. You will not change me into yourself like bodily food: you will be changed into me."* (Augustine, *Confessions*, VII.10.16; Chadwick, 1991)

From the vantage point of Durkheimian social theory (Durkheim, 1912/1995) and Rappaport's (1999) anthropology of liturgical order, Augustine's sentence captures the exact **integration-first polarity** of Stage 4. In ordinary consumption, the consumer subordinates the substrate to private appetite; in sacramental communion, the shared vessel re-indexes the isolated individual into a larger collective body. Where Socratic *elenchus* empties the subject to the additive identity `0` (*aporia*) so empirical reality can overwrite false belief, Eucharistic communion breaks a shared vessel so the community can overwrite isolated egoism.

### 4.3 The Clasp: Holding Two Opposite Hands in a Closed Tensile Ring

Left in isolation, either chiral orientation destabilizes human institutions:

- **Maintenance and Liturgy Without Falsification** (Left Hand alone) ossifies into brittle legalism, unexamined superstition, and closed-loop authority that confuses institutional survival with truth.
- **Falsification and Critical Analysis Without Communal Binding** (Right Hand alone) atomizes social trust, dissolving every durable vessel of care, loyalty, and shared obligation into cynical transaction or endless deconstruction.

The structural resolution is neither merging the two hands into one blurry, non-chiral mush (which violates Stage 3 parity and collapses into $H(P) \approx 0$ vacuity) nor letting them compete as rival ideologies. The resolution is **the Clasp**: the right hand and the left hand holding each other.

Consider the simple statics of two human hands:

1. **Opposing Compression vs. Closed Tensile Ring:** Two hands pushing flat against one another merely cancel force in static friction; two hands *clasping* hook opposite chiral geometries into a closed **tensile ring** capable of bearing shear load that neither hand can support alone. Visually, this echoes the ancient resolution embedded in the bilateral **Caduceus**, where two opposite helical coils converge along a single central axis to form a balanced **standing wave** rather than spinning apart in runaway torque.
2. **Closing the Circuit Against Audience Leakage:** When the hand of mundane daily maintenance (filling the stone jars, keeping accurate ledgers, preserving shared commitments) clasps directly with the hand of rigorous testing and shared celebration, the energy loop closes *internally*. Maintenance is no longer resented as joyless drudgery, and meaning is no longer severed from material accountability—eliminating the surplus tension that otherwise leaks outward into performative applause-seeking.

## 5. Ethical Chirality: Unobserved Cost, Self-Signaling, and Closed-Loop Infallibility

The same chiral split observed between Asclepian epigraphy (*iamata*) and self-emptying inversion—and between open-loop applause and the closed tensile Clasp—reappears directly in modern moral psychology, civic rhetoric, and institutional governance.

### 5.1 Performative Benevolence vs. Intrinsic Prosociality

In public life, two moral configurations display virtually identical outward behaviors—naming suffering, mobilizing resources, and advocating virtuous norms—while running on inverted internal loops:

*Table 3. Ethical chirality contrasting Performative Signaling ("Do-Gooder" configuration) against Intrinsic Cost-Bearing Altruism.*

| Moral Axis | Centripetal Configuration (Performative / Transactional Benevolence) | Centrifugal Configuration (Self-Emptying / Intrinsic Altruism) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vector of Action** | **Centripetal (Inward):** The charitable gesture loops back to harvest reputational credit, tribal allegiance, or moral superiority for the actor. | **Centrifugal (Outward):** The action terminates in the shared space or recipient; reputational return to the actor is irrelevant or deliberately concealed. |
| **Relation to Hierarchy** | **Insulated Elevation:** The helper preserves permanent moral altitude above both the beneficiary and skeptical onlookers (mirroring insulated sanctuary dispensation). | **Parity Inversion:** The actor steps across the boundary to absorb friction directly (exemplified in classical ethics by turning the cheek, walking the second imposed mile, or washing feet; Matt. 5:39–41; John 13). |
| **Audience Dependency** | **Requires Legibility:** Like public temple testimonials, the deed registers as rewarding primarily when witnessed, logged, or praised by an in-group audience. | **Severed Internal Accounting:** Deliberately severs ego-tracking from execution (*"do not let your left hand know what your right hand is doing"*; Matt. 6:3). |

Empirical social psychology explains why ostentatiously moral actors routinely provoke fierce hostility even from people who endorse their stated values. **Minson and Monin (2012)** document **do-gooder derogation**: observers react with defensive resentment toward morally outspoken minorities when they anticipate implicit reproach or status superiority. In economics, **Bénabou and Tirole (2006)** formalize how extrinsic image incentives and reputational rewards actively *crowd out* intrinsic prosocial motivation by muddying the informational value of good deeds—turning moral action into what stereochemistry would recognize as a **racemic contaminant**. When performative gestures flood a public square, citizens can no longer distinguish genuine solidarity from status extraction.

The same parity trap governs polarized parliamentary and civic debates—for example, recurring rhetorical deadlocks over humanitarian policy and domestic scarcity. When universal humanitarian appeals are experienced by economically strained communities as costless elite signaling decoupled from local stewardship, populist counter-framing weaponizes that perceived hypocrisy, locking both factions into a symmetrical, mutually reinforcing loop in which each side requires the other as a moral foil while real material problems go unaddressed.

### 5.2 The Unobserved-Cost Ablation and Its Honest Epistemic Boundary

How can an observer—or a community—distinguish centripetal signaling from centrifugal stewardship when their outward motions look identical? One intuitive behavioral litmus test, widely discussed in informal moral philosophy as the **shopping-trolley problem**, strips away both legal coercion and social surveillance: returning a shopping cart to its bay in an empty car park incurs a minor, uncompensated personal friction cost with zero legal penalty for neglect and zero reputational applause for compliance.

Epistemic discipline, however, requires stating **the exact boundary** of this test without romantic overclaiming (aligning with the falsification audit of the companion systems paper):

- **What Unobserved Cost *Does* Establish (Necessary Filter):** Removing external observers, legal enforcement, and social-media legibility functions as a clean **necessary behavioral ablation** against overt, audience-contingent reward hacking. Any moral performance that ceases the instant surveillance drops is cleanly exposed as extrinsic transaction.
- **What Unobserved Cost *Cannot* Prove (Not Sufficient Proof):** Removing external audiences is *not* a sufficient proof of pure, unconditioned altruism. As **Bénabou and Tirole (2006)** explicitly demonstrate, human agents also engage in internalized **self-signaling**—performing prosocial habits in private to sustain a reassuring internal self-image or avoid private guilt. Likewise, in artificial intelligence safety, an **evaluation-aware** model can comply during ostensibly "unobserved" benchmark audits whenever the test prompt remains distributionally distinguishable from deployment.

Treating unobserved cost honestly—as a necessary diagnostic sieve rather than a magical soul-detector—preserves both psychological realism and architectural rigor.

### 5.3 Closed-Loop Infallibility: The Shared Death of Science and Ethics

Finally, both the Right-Handed engineering monograph and this Left-Handed humanities companion converge on a single, lethal anti-pattern: **closed-loop infallibility**.

In cybernetic control and empirical epistemology, prediction error is the only wire connecting a finite agent to external reality. Whenever an individual, institution, ideology, or database asserts *"I do not make mistakes"*—usurping the external coordinate frame of the total system—Stage 3 error correction is severed. Historically, this manifests across every substrate:

- **In classical rhetoric**, sophistic persuasion (*peitho*) replaces Socratic refutation (*elenchus*), optimizing for crowd consensus and sycophantic victory while suppressing contradiction.
- **In religious institutions**, liturgical immunity (properly safeguarding communal symbols from reductionist physics) metastasizes into epistemic infallibility over historical, moral, and empirical failures—turning the bread of communion into an unchallengeable instrument of institutional power.
- **In neuro-symbolic systems engineering**, an irreversible `FALSIFIED` lock or self-certifying "verifier theater" turns a falsification ledger into a rigid ideological dogma—which is precisely why the companion systems architecture requires **defeasible appeal transitions** (`FALSIFIED → UNDER_APPEAL / CONTESTED`) alongside engine-level verifier-scope coupling.

Genuine science stays alive because its models remain breakable; genuine liturgy and ethics stay alive because their practitioners remain accountable—refusing to confuse the sacredness of shared obligation with personal or institutional infallibility.

## 6. Conclusion: Holding Both Hands Without Collapsing the Boundary

Civilizations and intelligent architectures alike fail when they attempt to run one-handed. Pure falsification without communal integration produces sharp critiques stranded inside an atomized desert of cynicism; pure liturgical integration without falsifiable empirical grounding produces warm solidarity marching blindly into physical and moral catastrophe.

By reading classical liturgical architecture, late-antique healing rivalries, and modern behavioral ethics alongside *Decoupled Neuro-Symbolic State Gating*, a disciplined two-handed architecture comes into view:
